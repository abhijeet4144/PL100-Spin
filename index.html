🦁 PL 100 — How to Play

Build the greatest Premier League XI in history

Objective

  • Assemble a squad that simulates to 100+ points across a 38-game season — matching Man City's all-time record.

Formation

  • Choose from 4-4-2, 4-3-3, 4-2-3-1, 3-4-3 or 3-5-2 before you start spinning
  • Goalkeeper — Clean Sheets & Save %
  • Defenders — Clean Sheets, Tackles & Interceptions
  • Midfielders — Pass Acc %, Tackles, Interceptions, Chances Created, Goals & Assists
  • Forwards — Goals, Assists & Chances Created

The Spin

  • Each spin randomly reveals a player from the Premier League era (1992–2024), shown with their club and season.
  • Press Add to Team to place them in your squad.
  • Press Skip to pass — but you only get 5 skips per game. Use them wisely.
  • If a slot is already full or you've hit the club limit, the spin is free — it won't cost you a skip.

Squad Rules

  • Maximum 4 players from any one Premier League club.
  • You can remove a player from the pitch by hovering their slot (or tapping on mobile) and clicking ✕, freeing up the position.
  • Once all 11 slots are filled, hit Simulate Season.

Simulation

  • Your team's stats are converted into Attack, Defense and Control ratings.
  • A 38-game season is simulated using a Poisson goal model (20 runs averaged).
  • Results are compared against Premier League history.

Can you reach 100 points?

Spin to draw players from the Premier League era. Build a team of 11 that could dominate a 38-game season and achieve the legendary 100-point mark.

How to Play

  • Each spin reveals a player from a specific PL team and season
  • Choose a formation and build your XI: 1 GK + 10 outfield players
  • Accept or reject each player — rejected spins still count
  • Once your squad is full, simulate the season
  • Reach 100 points to join the century club
  • Stats drawn from Opta data across the Premier League era
